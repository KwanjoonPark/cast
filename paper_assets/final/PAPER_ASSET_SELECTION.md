Paper story: Dense → Entmax sparse cross-attention → PLADIS extrapolation → interaction with guidance strength. On one GPU, PLADIS never outperforms Dense at any of four guidance settings; its penalty decreases consistently as guidance is weakened; at CFG 1.5 and without CFG, AQAScore and PQ are statistically indistinguishable from Dense while CLAP retains a residual loss. Allowed phrasing: "suggests an interaction between classifier-free guidance strength and sparse cross-attention"; no causal claim, no generalisation beyond Auffusion.
